    Biosphera Scientific Guide & Hotkeys

    Neural Brain & Genetics

    Each organism processes sensory stimuli through a feedforward network. Offspring inherit mutated synaptic weights with an 8% mutation rate.

    Emergent Natural Selection

    Complex behaviors (flocking, evasion, ambush hunting) are not hardcoded; they emerge spontaneously as survival pressures weed out inefficient traits.

    Lotka-Volterra Cycles

    Predators consume herbivores; as prey dwindles, predators starve; as predator counts drop, herbivores rebound.

    Currents, Seasons & Scent

    A slowly evolving flow field pushes everything through the water. Four seasons scale vegetation growth and metabolic burn. Organisms leave chemical trails while feeding and dying; descendants with a 9th "scent" neural input can evolve trail-following and danger avoidance.

    Sexual Reproduction

    Toggle Mating in God Mode: offspring then require a willing partner nearby and inherit a recombined genome (each trait from either parent, whole neurons per parent) plus mutation. Parthenogenesis returns automatically when the population collapses below 12.
